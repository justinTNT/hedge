Assign curator access
  1. Ask the person to sign in to this site once.
  2. Open Identity, find their name or email, and copy Provider and ProviderUserId.
  3. Open Grant → New, enter those values, set Role to curator, tick Enabled, and save. GrantedBy is optional.
  4. They can then sign in and open Review contributions. They do not need your admin key.

Untick Enabled to revoke access. Identities are read-only; use the provider account ID, not an identity or guest record ID. Curators review corrections and offered photographs; they cannot edit the catalogue or assign roles.
